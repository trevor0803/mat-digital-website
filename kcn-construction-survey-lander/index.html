FAQ

What homeowners usually want to know first.

What kind of projects does KCN handle?

This lander is focused on remodeling and general contracting. The exact scope is confirmed after KCN reviews the project details and property.

What areas does KCN serve?

KCN serves projects across Northern Virginia, Washington DC and Maryland. Availability depends on project type, location and schedule.

Does submitting the form give me an instant estimate?

No. The form is an estimate request. KCN follows up to understand the project and determine the appropriate next step before pricing is finalized.

What should I include in the project description?

A short summary is enough: what you want to change, the property ZIP code, and any important goals or conditions KCN should know about.
