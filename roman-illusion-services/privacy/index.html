Privacy Policy

Effective August 7, 2026

Roman Illusion, Inc. ("Roman Illusion," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit our website, request an estimate, contact us, or choose to communicate with us by phone, email, or text message.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly to us, including your name, phone number, email address, project ZIP code, project details, service preferences, scheduling information, and any comments you submit. We may also collect limited technical information such as browser type, device information, pages visited, and similar website usage data.

How We Use Information

We may use information to respond to estimate requests, schedule appointments, communicate about painting or coating projects, provide customer service, maintain business records, improve our website and services, and protect against fraud or misuse. If you separately opt in to SMS, we may also use your mobile number for the text-message purposes described at the point of consent.

SMS and Mobile Information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

If you opt in to SMS from Roman Illusion, Inc., message frequency varies based on your estimate request, scheduling needs, and project communications. Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP or contact us at (610) 991-6438 or romanillusionpro@gmail.com. Consent to receive SMS is not a condition of purchase.

How We Share Information

We may disclose information to vendors and service providers that perform services for us, such as website hosting, communications, scheduling, customer management, analytics, or other business operations. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a business transaction. We do not sell mobile opt-in information or SMS consent for third-party marketing.

Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support functionality, understand traffic, and improve the site. Your browser may allow you to control certain cookie settings.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, to maintain business and consent records, and to satisfy legal, accounting, or operational requirements.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You may contact us to request correction or deletion of certain personal information, subject to applicable legal and business-record requirements. You may unsubscribe from SMS at any time by replying STOP. You may also contact us directly regarding communications preferences.

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults seeking residential or commercial painting and related services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above reflects the current version.
