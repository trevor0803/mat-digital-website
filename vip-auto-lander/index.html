Lease or finance?

Why a lease usually means a lower monthly payment.

A car loan pays off the full purchase price of the vehicle. A lease only covers the depreciation across your term plus a finance charge, which is why leasing usually produces a lower payment than financing the same car.

A standard lease term usually sits inside the factory warranty, so covered repairs stay with the manufacturer. If you would rather drive something newer every few years on a predictable payment, leasing is usually cheaper and simpler.

How a lease payment is built

  1. Start with the price of the car.
  2. Subtract the residual value it is expected to hold at the end of your term.
  3. Spread the difference across the months of the lease.
  4. Add the money factor, which works like the interest rate on a loan.

Where we save you money: lower the starting price and the whole payment falls with it. That starting price is what we negotiate.
