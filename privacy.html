Privacy Policy

How KCN Construction collects, uses and protects information submitted through its website and text messaging program.

Last updated: August 6, 2026

This policy applies to information collected through the KCN Construction website, estimate forms, telephone calls, emails and SMS conversations.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, email address, mobile phone number, project address or city, project details, photos, preferred contact method and other information you choose to provide.

How we use information

We use information to respond to estimate requests, communicate about scheduling and appointments, provide project and customer-service updates, prepare proposals, improve our services, maintain business records and comply with legal obligations.

SMS and mobile information

When you voluntarily opt in, KCN Construction may send SMS messages regarding your estimate request, scheduling, appointment reminders, project updates and customer support. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time and HELP for help.

Mobile information will not be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be shared with service providers only as necessary to support requested communications and business operations.

SMS consent is optional and is not a condition of purchasing goods or services from KCN Construction.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information and retain it only as long as reasonably necessary for business, legal and service purposes.

Your choices

To stop SMS messages, reply STOP. For help, reply HELP, call (703) 317-7237, or email info@kcnconstructiondmv.com.
