Privacy Policy

How KCN Construction collects, uses and protects information submitted through its website and text messaging program.

Last updated: August 6, 2026

This policy applies to information collected through the KCN Construction website, estimate forms, telephone calls, emails and SMS conversations.

1. Information we collect

We may collect your name, email address, mobile phone number, project address or city, project details, photos, preferred contact method and other information you choose to provide. We may also collect basic technical information such as browser type, device type and website usage data.

2. How we use information

We use information to respond to estimate requests, communicate about scheduling and appointments, provide project and customer-service updates, prepare proposals, improve our services, maintain business records and comply with legal obligations.

3. SMS and mobile information

When you voluntarily opt in, KCN Construction may send SMS messages regarding your estimate request, scheduling, appointment reminders, project updates and customer support. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time and HELP for help.

Mobile information will not be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except service providers that are necessary to deliver the requested messaging service and are prohibited from using the information for their own marketing.

SMS consent is optional and is not a condition of purchasing goods or services from KCN Construction.

4. How information may be disclosed

We may disclose information to service providers that help us operate the website, communicate with customers, host data or perform business functions. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a business transfer. We do not authorize service providers to use mobile opt-in information for unrelated marketing.

5. Cookies and analytics

The website may use cookies or similar technologies for basic functionality, security, traffic measurement and performance. You may control cookies through your browser settings.

6. Data security and retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information. No system is completely secure. We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy and applicable business or legal requirements.

7. Your choices

You may request access, correction or deletion of personal information by contacting us. To stop SMS messages, reply STOP. For help, reply HELP, call (703) 317-7237, or email info@kcnconstructiondmv.com.

8. Children’s privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and KCN Construction does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated date shown above indicates the latest revision.
